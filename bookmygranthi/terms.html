Terms of use

Last updated November 2026. This is a starting template and should be reviewed by a legal professional before launch.

What BookMyGranthi is

BookMyGranthi is an online directory and request service that helps families find granthis and paathis. We are not a party to any arrangement between a family and a granthi, and we do not conduct ceremonies.

Booking requests

Sending a request does not guarantee a booking. A booking is confirmed only when the granthi accepts it. Please confirm all details directly with the granthi.

Seva

Amounts shown on profiles are the granthi’s suggested seva and are a guide only. Seva is agreed and settled directly between you and the granthi. BookMyGranthi does not charge families a booking fee and does not process payments for ceremonies.

Cancellations

Please cancel as early as you can so the granthi can offer the date to another family. You can cancel a request from “My bookings”, or contact the granthi directly.

Granthis

Granthis are independent. Profiles are provided by the granthi, and “Verified” means identity and a reference have been checked; it is not a guarantee of any particular outcome. Please let us know if anything on a profile is inaccurate.

Acceptable use

Please use the service respectfully and provide accurate information. We may remove listings or restrict access where the service is misused.
