About us

Finding a granthi shouldn’t depend on who you happen to know.

For generations, families have found a granthi through word of mouth: a call to an uncle, a message in a family group, a question to the gurdwara. That works until you move cities, lose touch, or need someone urgently. We’re building a simple, respectful alternative.

What we do

BookMyGranthi is an online directory and booking service. Families can find granthis and paathis near them, see what ceremonies they offer, in which languages and when they’re free, and send a request in a few minutes.

We don’t conduct ceremonies and we don’t take the place of your gurdwara or community. We simply make it easier for the right person and the right family to find each other.

What we believe

  • Seva comes first. A granthi’s time is Sewa. We show suggested amounts so families can plan, and we never add booking fees for families.
  • Maryada matters. Profiles are clear about the ceremonies offered, so there are no surprises on the day.
  • Every family belongs. Whether you’re in your hometown or halfway across the world, a granthi who speaks your language should be easy to find.
  • Calm in hard moments. For funerals and urgent needs, we prioritise speed and gentleness over everything else.

How we verify

Granthis marked Verified have had their identity checked and have given a reference from a gurdwara or community, and we review feedback from families. Where a granthi hasn’t completed verification, their profile says so.
