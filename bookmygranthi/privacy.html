Privacy

Last updated November 2026. This is a starting template and should be reviewed by a legal professional before launch.

What we collect

When you send a booking request, join as a granthi or contact us, we collect the details you enter: your name, email, phone number, ceremony details (date, venue, notes) and, for granthis, your experience and references.

How we use it

  • To pass your booking request to the granthi you chose, and to let you and them confirm and plan.
  • To verify granthis who apply to join.
  • To reply to your messages and improve the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who sees it

Your details are shared only with the granthi you send a request to, and with the small team that operates BookMyGranthi. We may use trusted service providers (such as email delivery) that process data on our behalf.

Cookies and storage

This site stores your booking drafts and requests in your browser’s local storage so you can return to them. It does not use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us.

Retention

We keep booking records only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations.
